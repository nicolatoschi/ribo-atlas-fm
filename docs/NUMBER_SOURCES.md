# Where each number in the proposal comes from

The proposal documents (Project Scope and Plan, online form text and evidence companion, 16 Oct 2026) are generated by builders that import `resource_plan/facts_v2.json`. Every number inserted from that file traces to the facts file by construction, and `build_facts_v2.py` reads most of those values from the retrieved analyses, ledgers and rank reports listed under its `sources`.

This page covers the remaining numbers: those typed literally in the document text. It also covers the few values that the document builder computes from facts (trajectory durations, the FL01-993 subtotal, the flagged share). We found them by parsing every string literal in the document builders, which are not part of this repository. We then checked the result against the text of the current PDFs.

`tools/check_number_sources.py --evidence-root <pack>/evidence` recomputes every item marked "checked" from the execution pack. All 66 checks pass.

## C. Design and plan parameters (stated choices, not measurements)

**Recorded in `design_v2.json` and checked:**
- fixed allowances of 3,000 NH (1,500 references and preprocessing, 1,000 fitting, 500 operations);
- 80% evaluation packing;
- a 300-case development panel;
- data-scaling fractions of 10% and 30%;
- 3 arms × 5 seeds, 300,000 and 50,000 cases, and 3 and 8 passes (these also reach the documents through facts).

**Stated in the plan only:**
- H1 effect targets: at least 5% lower probe error and at least 0.02 lDDT, 95% intervals, a validity bound of −0.01 and an 80% power target;
- continuation thresholds of 1% and 0.005 lDDT;
- lDDT radii: 15 Å, with tolerances of 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 Å;
- the per-job throughput screen threshold of 15%;
- production job shapes: 64- and 128-node jobs, 12-hour segments, four-node trajectories of 16 GPUs × 8 RNAs = batch 128, up to 32 per 128-node job, 256/512 GPUs per job;
- checkpoints every 30 minutes, with a maximum/desirable interval of 1 h / 0.5 h;
- storage requests (SCRATCH 2 TB, split 0.7/0.5/0.4/0.4; WORK 0.5 TB; HOME 0.02 TB), file-count ceilings (100,000, 20,000 and 10,000; 5,000 transient) and transfer volumes (1.5 TB as 0.3 + 4 × 0.1 + 0.8);
- the host-memory budget of 128 GB per node, and the per-job memory derived from it (512, 8,192 and 16,384 GB);
- CPU cores per job (18,432 and 36,864, from 288 cores per JUPITER node);
- risk scales (1–5, probability bands 10/25/50/75%) and priority scores (16, 15, 8);
- milestone months;
- the s/h conversion (3,600) and rounding to 100.
